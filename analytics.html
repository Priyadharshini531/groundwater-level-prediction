Data Transformation & Preprocessing Architecture

Before inference scoring, raw hydrostatic telemetry undergoes three sequential conditioning filters to eliminate sensor drift, handle quarterly sparseness, and normalize features:

filter_alt Hierarchical Imputation

Station historical mean fallback for initial quarters followed by district-level quarterly seasonal mean.

timeline Auto-Regressive Lags

Engineered Lag_1 (prior quarter) and Lag_4 (same quarter 1-yr prior) to capture multi-year persistence.

straighten Trigonometric Cycles

Sin/Cos month and quarter transformations to model periodic NE/SW monsoon replenishment cycles.

verified Documentation Standard: CGWB Tier-II Hydrogeological Assessment
